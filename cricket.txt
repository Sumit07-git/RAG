# 🏏 The Beautiful Game of Cricket

A long poem about passion, dreams, teamwork, and the magic of cricket

The sun rises high, the sky turns blue, The field awaits, the grass shines with dew. The pitch is ready, the stumps stand tall, The players walk in, prepared for it all.

The crowd begins with a thunderous cheer, The sound of cricket fills the atmosphere. With flags held high and hearts beating fast, They hope this moment forever will last.

The batsman arrives with a dream in his eyes, A thousand hopes beneath the skies. He holds his bat with a steady hand, Ready to fight, ready to stand.

The bowler takes a deep breath in, Determined and focused, hoping to win. He marks his run-up, turns around, Then races forward across the ground.

The ball leaves his hand, so quick and bright, The batsman prepares for the coming fight. A swing of the bat, a beautiful sound, The ball races fast along the ground.

A boundary! The crowd begins to roar, The fans shout loudly, asking for more. The next ball rises, soaring so high, A mighty six into the sky!

The stadium shakes with endless delight, As thousands of voices unite. Some wave their flags, some dance with joy, Cricket brings happiness to every girl and boy.

But cricket is more than hitting a ball, It's learning to rise whenever we fall. It's keeping our courage when times are tough, And believing in ourselves when the road gets rough.

Sometimes the wickets fall one by one, And suddenly the game seems almost done. The scoreboard shows a difficult chase, Yet hope still shines on every face.

A captain gathers the team around, His words bring strength without a sound. "Believe in yourselves, the game isn't through, The greatest victories begin with you."

The fielders spread across the green, Focused and ready, sharp and keen. One dives forward, another runs back, Each player ready to cover the gap.

A ball flies high, a catch is near, The crowd falls silent, gripped by fear. A fielder leaps with all his might, And catches the ball — what a wonderful sight!

The batter walks back, his innings done, The bowler celebrates what he has won. But even in defeat, there is something to learn, For every lost battle helps ambition burn.

The afternoon passes, the shadows grow, The pressure increases with each ball's throw. The required runs climb, the overs decline, And every second feels like a sign.

The final over, six balls to play, The team needs fifteen to win the day. The batsman stands with nerves of steel, Knowing the weight of what he must feel.

The first ball comes, he swings with grace, The ball finds the gap and wins the race. Four runs! The crowd comes alive, The dream of victory begins to thrive.

The second ball flies beyond the rope, The fans jump up with renewed hope. A mighty six lights up the sky, And joyful voices rise up high.

The third ball comes, a slower delivery, The batsman defends with great bravery. A dot ball adds to the tension and fear, But the captain shouts, "The finish is near!"

The fourth ball races towards the bat, He guides it away — imagine that! Two runs taken, they hurry back, The fielders throw along the track.

Two balls remain, three runs to win, The bowler wipes the sweat from his skin. The batsman looks toward the stands, And tightens his grip with steady hands.

The bowler runs in, the whole world still, The ball comes fast with incredible skill. The batsman swings with all his might, The ball sails high, a magnificent flight!

It clears the boundary, far away, A six to finish the thrilling day! The crowd erupts, the flags fly high, As happy tears fill every eye.

The teammates run to celebrate, A moment that will become their fate. They hug and laugh beneath the sun, For together, they have won!

And somewhere among that cheering crowd, A young fan dreams of making them proud. He holds a bat made of wood, Believing one day he'll be great and good.

He plays in the streets when the day is done, Beneath the moon or the evening sun. A wall becomes a wicket, a stone becomes a ball, And dreams grow bigger with every call.

His friends take turns, they laugh and play, They imagine the stadium they'll see one day. No grand equipment, no shining ground, Just endless passion all around.

He misses a shot, he falls to the floor, But quickly gets up and asks for one more. He learns that success takes patience and time, And every small effort helps him climb.

For cricket belongs to the rich and poor, To every child behind every door. It crosses the borders of language and land, Bringing people together, hand in hand.

It teaches respect for the game and the foe, To accept the result, whatever may show. To shake hands proudly when the match is through, And give your best in all that you do.

From village fields to stadium lights, From early mornings to thrilling nights, The love of cricket continues to grow, Like a mighty river with a powerful flow.

Some love the bat and the runs they score, Some love the bowlers who give their all. Some cheer for catches, some for the spin, And some love the drama of a narrow win.

There are legends whose names will never fade, Whose fearless moments history has made. Their records inspire the young and old, Their stories are treasured more than gold.

Yet every player, famous or new, Must face the same skies of changing blue. The game rewards those who dare to try, Who stand back up when their dreams run dry.

And when the stadium lights grow dim, The memories remain within. The cheers may fade, the crowds depart, But cricket lives forever in the heart.

So let the bat swing, let the ball fly, Let every player reach for the sky. Let every child chase a dream so bright, And never surrender without a fight.

For cricket is passion, cricket is pride, A bond that keeps us all united. It's laughter, courage, hope, and glory, A game that writes a thousand stories.

And when the final ball is bowled one day, When the last cheer slowly fades away, One truth will remain, forever true:

Cricket is not just a game we play, It's a beautiful feeling that never fades away. 🏏❤️
